About BS Law

Bandar Alzahrani is a Saudi-qualified lawyer and the Founder and Managing Attorney of BS Law. He advises local and international clients on a broad range of commercial and civil matters, with particular experience in commercial law, dispute resolution, anti-money laundering regulations, intellectual property, trademark registration, appeals, and estate planning.

Bandar is admitted to practice in Saudi Arabia and holds an LL.B. from Umm Al-Qura University and an LL.M. in International Banking and Finance Law from the University of Leeds.
